# Demo video plan — 3 minutes

One-line pitch: Strongo is community-run food delivery that takes 0% —
order in plain words, drivers claim work in the open, every dollar audited.

Target: under 3:00 (participant-reported requirement; confirm in the
submission portal). Record at 1080p with captions. Preload: one open
order, seeded hubs and needs. If `MODEL_API_KEY` is live, the AI badges
show; otherwise the local-mode badges show honestly — either is fine.

## Shots

- 0:00–0:12 `/` — Landing hero "Neighbors feeding neighbors", receipt
  ledger, 15–30% / 59% / 0% stats. "Delivery apps take thirty percent.
  Strongo is community-run delivery that takes zero."
- 0:12–0:35 `/delivery` — Type "Feed my family of 4 for under $40",
  Suggest, show the badge, Use this suggestion, address, Place order.
  "Ask in plain words. The AI dispatcher builds the cart; the restaurant
  keeps one hundred percent."
- 0:35–0:55 `/dispatch` — Pick a driver, Accept delivery, Mark picked up,
  Mark delivered. "Drivers claim work in the open, with fixed published
  stipends — no algorithm pay cuts."
- 0:55–1:08 `/transparency` — Where-every-dollar-goes table plus the
  saved-vs-30% row. "Open books. Every order shows where every dollar
  goes."
- 1:08–1:22 `/menu-scan` — Menu photo, Scan, review items, Publish.
  "Restaurants join with one photo — AI transcribes the paper menu, no
  tablets or fees."
- 1:22–1:40 `/pilot` — Portland map, flip Customer/Driver toggle, open
  deliveries and demand lists. "One map for workers and neighbors —
  see the work or see the food."
- 1:40–1:55 `/hubs` — Service filter, Open now only, night board post
  and claim. "Food, tools, rides, and a night board that never closes."
- 1:55–2:15 `/garden` — Address, plan, crop cards, totals, coach
  question. "Then beyond food: turn a yard into a garden plan."
- 2:15–2:35 `/simulate` — Livestock schedule and dog-corridor costs.
  "And we show the math on wild ideas."
- 2:35–2:55 `/research/*` — Docs index, close on the roadmap. "Documented
  in the open — Strongo, built for your world."
